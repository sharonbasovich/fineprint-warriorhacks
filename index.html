How it works

  1. Reads the letter in your browser. Text extraction happens locally — nothing is uploaded, and no AI service is called.
  2. Finds what the letter asks. Renewal methods, due-date wording, the checked programs, and each required or conditional document — conditions and exceptions are quoted word-for-word.
  3. Links every answer to its sentence. Each checklist item shows the exact quote and page it came from.
  4. Quotes before it answers. A date only becomes a "Respond by" card when the letter's own words give an unambiguous present-tense deadline instruction; anything historical, mixed or unclear is shown as a quoted candidate instead.
  5. Admits what isn't there. Each finding is marked as stated, unclear, or not said — a blank due date stays "not stated" and an unchecked program is not assumed covered.

Scope: Fineprint is an evidence reader for benefits-renewal notices in the style of Texas form H1830-R. Text that doesn't look like one gets an honest unsupported notice and no checklist. It is a document-comprehension demo, not legal aid and not a government service — it makes no eligibility, medical, or legal decisions. Synthetic samples are clearly labeled; the bundled official sample is the blank 2018 Texas HHSC form linked in the source manifest.
